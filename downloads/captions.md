## L01 — The staff-room secret

**LinkedIn**

When people ask me who joins our Master of Arts in Education, I give an answer that surprises them.

More than 6 in 10 of our past students were ACA educators.

They taught full days. They planned, marked, ran clubs and parent meetings. And every week, they came to class to work toward a Saint Mary's University of Minnesota degree.

That tells me two things. First, the programme works around a real teaching schedule. Second, the best advertisement for professional learning is a colleague who has done it.

If you've been thinking about a master's, ask someone in your staff room who has done it. Then ask me anything.

Our January 2027 cohort is open → https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/GC5UUQubjU

## L02 — What I learned from our cohorts

**LinkedIn**

Three things I've learned from coordinating our MAED cohorts:

1. Nobody feels "ready". The educators who finish are the ones who start anyway.
2. The cohort is the curriculum too. Teachers from Canada, the US, the UK, South Africa, Lebanon, Jordan, Tunisia and Kuwait learn as much from each other as from the course.
3. Small, monthly steps beat one big leap. Eighteen months goes faster than you think.

If one of these sounds like you, our next cohort starts on 15 March.
