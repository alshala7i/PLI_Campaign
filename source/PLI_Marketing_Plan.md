# PART 1 — STRATEGY

## 1.1 The situation in one paragraph
PLI sells two Saint Mary's University of Minnesota degrees delivered in Kuwait: the **MAED** (Master of Arts in Education, Curriculum & Instruction or Educational Leadership, 30 credits / 10 courses / 18 months) and the **Ed.D** (Executive Leadership Doctorate, 39 credits / 3 years / fully online / no dissertation). About **62% of past students were ACA staff** and **38% were external**, from at least eight nationalities. The current Instagram account looks good but talks mostly about events (Saturday Learning Series) and programme facts. It rarely uses its strongest proof point (most students are ACA educators), never shows the monthly price, and has no clear path from "interested" to "applied". The website "Apply Now" buttons also point to ACA job listings, not to a PLI application. So the campaign must create demand **and** build the path to conversion.

## 1.2 The big idea — "Grow Where You Teach"
The 62% statistic is the campaign's anchor. It does three jobs at once:
1. **Social proof for ACA staff:** "Your colleagues already did it." The most persuasive message for a teacher is that someone in the same staff room, with the same workload, finished it.
2. **Credibility for external educators:** an international school chose this programme for its own people. That is an endorsement.
3. **Feasibility:** if full-time ACA teachers manage it, the schedule works.

**Master line:** Grow where you teach.
**Online variant:** Grow wherever you teach.
**Ed.D variant:** Lead the next chapter.
**Proof line (use everywhere):** More than 6 in 10 of our past MAED students were ACA educators.

## 1.4 Positioning and message hierarchy
Use the messages in this order. Every piece of content leads with one and supports it with another.

1. **Proof:** Your colleagues already did it (62% ACA).
2. **Built for working teachers:** weekly in-person classes in Kuwait, 18 months, keep teaching.
3. **Simple money:** pay monthly from 222.22 KD (ACA staff) / 233.33 KD (external) · PL stipend + signing bonus (ACA staff) · referral reward.
4. **Credibility:** Saint Mary's University of Minnesota, accredited in the US by the Higher Learning Commission.
5. **Reach:** online option for anyone outside Kuwait, leading to the same degree.

**Tone of voice:** warm, direct, colleague-to-colleague. Short sentences. Talk to "you", not "candidates". No hype, no exclamation-mark overload, no promises of promotions or salary rises. International English (the audience is Canadian, American, British, South African, Lebanese, Jordanian, Tunisian, Kuwaiti…).

## 1.11 Claims and compliance guardrails (must follow)

| Topic | ✅ Say | ❌ Never say |

| Kuwait recognition | "Recognized by Kuwait's Ministry of Education as part of work-permit approval." (**wording to be approved by PLI/HR**) | Anything implying Civil Service Commission recognition, salary-scale recognition or equivalency for Kuwaiti citizens |

| Online option | "Same programme. Same degree. Same diploma." | "No one will know you studied online" or anything that sounds like hiding it |

## 5.3 Tech stack
- Node.js 20+, TypeScript, pnpm or npm.
- **Static posts, carousels, stories, covers, posters, guide:** HTML/CSS templates rendered with **Playwright (Chromium)** at exact pixel sizes (`deviceScaleFactor: 1`). PDFs via Playwright `page.pdf` with exact width/height or by merging PNGs with `pdf-lib`. QR codes with `qrcode`.
- **Fonts:** download Anton, Playfair Display and Montserrat (Google Fonts, OFL) into `fonts/` and load locally with `@font-face` (no network at render time). Wait for `document.fonts.ready` before every screenshot.
- **Videos:** **Remotion** (React). One generic `<Scene>` system driven by the scene tables in Part 3.1 (on-screen text, motion type, duration). If the user's Claude Code has a motion-video skill or plugin installed, it may be used instead, as long as this spec is met.
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs Text-to-Speech **with timestamps** endpoint (character alignment) → derive word timings → drive caption timing and scene lengths from the real audio. Model: the current multilingual/high-quality ElevenLabs model available to the key. Voice: warm, professional, neutral international/North American English (pick one voice and use it for all 16 videos for consistency; save the choice in `config/voice.json`).
- **Audio mix:** VO at about −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP; music bed ducked ~−20 dB under VO, 0.5 s fade in/out.
- **Encoding:** H.264 (yuv420p), AAC 48 kHz, 30 fps; `-preset veryfast`; `+faststart`.
- **Spreadsheet:** `exceljs` for `calendar.xlsx` (frozen header, filters, column widths, Kuwait dates).
